Who we are

A Gateway to Revival, Restoration & Glory

Welcome to Gateway Revival Glory Church and Ministries (GRGC&M) — a loving, Spirit-filled family headquartered at Corner House, Nakasero, Entebbe Road, Kampala, under the visionary leadership of Pastor Agatha Christine Idusso (“Mama Agatha”). Since our launch on 17th August 2013, we have witnessed salvation of lost souls, healings, deliverance, restoration & prosperity — and grown to 3 branches: the City Church, Tema (Kikyusa–Tema) and Kayabwe on Masaka Road.

We are a family that shares good and bad moments — a movement that follows the leading of the Spirit, preaching purity, holiness and righteousness, and embracing all forms of prayer as our pivot. We did not choose Him, but He chose us (John 15:16–17).

  • Spiritual counselling & prayer for everyone
  • Tools for building God's kingdom through Christ's love
  • Core values: love, fellowship, faithfulness, unity, purity, holiness, compassion, integrity
  • A people's church — every gift of the Spirit is honoured

Our Journey — from a dream in 1989 to 12 years of glory

  1. 1989 — The first commissionTwo years after receiving Christ, Agatha hears her name called three times in a dream: “Build Me a church.”
  2. 1990s — ObedienceWhile still a public civil servant, she buys materials and puts up a semi-permanent church building.
  3. 2000 — Ministry of HelpsAn audible voice: “Cook for dogs.” She begins cooking and feeding street children from her own vehicle.
  4. 2003 — Mother-to-Mother (M2M)Praying mothers gather every first Saturday to intercede for children, families, schools and the nation — on to this day.
  5. 2011 — “This is a gateway!”An angel in a night vision at Corner House, Nakasero: “Agatha, this is a gateway of revival and God's glory!” Apostle Charles Tumwine prophesies: begin the ministry.
  6. 17 Aug 2013 — LaunchGRGC is launched by Apostles Charles Tumwine & John Bunjo, Pastor Peter Michael Oumo and Dr. John Mulinde.
  7. Dec 2023 — Kikyusa–Tema branchA sister church opens under eucalyptus trees in Luweero; crusades, healings and reconciliation follow.
  8. 30 Aug 2025 — 12th Anniversary“Not to us, O Lord…” (Psalm 115:1) — thanksgiving, testimonies and our first anniversary magazine.

A Word from the Vision Bearer

❤ Flagship Project

The Children's Home in Gayaza

Our beating heart. The church has built a home for teenagers in Gayaza — rescuing, rehabilitating and housing former street children with meals, clothing, counselling, schooling and, above all, the love of Jesus. It is lovingly run by caretaker Mama Mirembe Immaculate, our intercessor, warden and welfare minister.

  • Safe housing, daily meals & medical care
  • School fees, uniforms & scholastic materials
  • Counselling, discipleship & life-skills training
  • Reunification with traced families where possible

✝️ The Gospel of Actions — Matthew 25:31–46

“I was a stranger and you welcomed me”

Shanga Judith, a Kenyan national, was found in Kasese — naked and eating from a dustbin. Missionary Don Foster brought her to Pastor Agatha in 2021; the church treated her at Butabika Hospital and, after 6+ years away, brought her home to reunite with her mother in Kenya.

Sumayah walked into a service wrapped in polythene bags. She accepted Christ, was treated at Butabika, gave birth — and reunited with her family in Mukono. This is church beyond words.
