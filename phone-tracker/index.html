How it works

  1. Create your account here (email + password). Only you can see your data.
  2. On the phone you want to protect, open this app, sign in and press Protect → Register & start protection. Allow location access when asked.
  3. Install it to the home screen (Add to Home Screen) so it keeps running like an app.
  4. From any other device (computer, friend's phone), sign in to Find to see the live map, ring the alarm, lock the screen, send a message or turn on stolen mode (fast tracking + wake lock).

What you can do remotely

  • 🚨 Ring alarm — a loud siren for 30 s (also vibrates).
  • 📍 Locate now — request an immediate GPS fix.
  • 🔒 Lock screen — fullscreen lock with your PIN; unlock it yourself or remotely.
  • ✉️ Message — show a fullscreen message to whoever has the phone.
  • ⚠️ Stolen mode — fast updates, keeps the screen awake, shows your message.
  • 📋 History with CSV / GPX export, Google Maps & WhatsApp sharing.

Honest limitations

  • This is a web app: browsers throttle background pages. Tracking is best while the screen is on (the app requests a screen wake-lock in stolen mode). It cannot run fully invisibly like a native anti-theft app.
  • The Protect tab must have been opened on the phone — if the thief (or you) fully closes the browser, remote commands wait until it opens again.
  • iPhone: vibration and alarms are silenced when the ringer switch is off, and the screen may sleep. Android gives the best results.
  • GPS is weakest indoors — the last known position may be metres off.

Security & privacy

  • Every row is protected by Postgres row-level security tied to your login — nobody else (not even the public API key on this page) can read your locations.
  • The unlock PIN is stored as a salted hash. Use a strong Supabase password and consider enabling MFA on your account in the Supabase dashboard.
  • Tips: keep the phone charged, share your live last-known location with family via WhatsApp from the Find tab, and report the IMEI to your carrier and police.
